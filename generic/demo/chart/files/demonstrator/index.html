2Generate workload and watch it flow

The tenants' apps log all the time. Pick a tenant and give its app more to say: its pod writes the extra lines to stdout (the agent on its node reads them from the node's disk) or sends them over OTLP (like an app with an OpenTelemetry SDK). Watch them cross every hop, pod by pod. Per second, measured every 3 s from each component's own metrics.

6Demo vs. production

Same components and data path as the production charts (generic/charts), at toy size: one namespace, Kafka without TLS/SCRAM, derived keys, local Grafana users, SeaweedFS instead of Blob/S3, one or two replicas of most things. Like production, every flow is allowed explicitly (here: CiliumNetworkPolicies on a default-deny cluster) and the read gateway runs on kgateway. The production version adds per-component namespaces, TLS, Key Vault or generated secrets, SSO, alerts and sizing (docs/01, docs/10).
